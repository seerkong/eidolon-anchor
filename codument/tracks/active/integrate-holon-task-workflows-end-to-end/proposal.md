# Track: Holon task workflows end to end

## Problem

The preceding library Tracks create necessary authorities but do not prove the actual product can author, publish, start, recover and inspect a complex organization-owned task through both AI Ctrl and AI Data workflows.

## Change

- Adopt exact released Holarchy File-XNL, TaskSpace, depa-actor and workflow package versions.
- Build one complete organization through the real File-XNL authority, issue its canonical snapshot/receipt, and admit those issuer outputs with execution binding, Agents, materials and Ctrl/Data workflows into one physical ResourcePackage.
- Exercise TaskSpace creation, HolonCoordinator assignment, claim and settlement through shared MemberRuntime reuse.
- Consume settlement in FlowRunCheckpoint through a separate durable CAS handoff.
- Prove File-XNL organization source drift or deletion is ignored by an admitted task until explicit replan.
- Expose bounded CLI/TUI status and receipts without physical paths or histories.

## Non-goals

- Provider-specific semantic routing or host keyword logic.
- Database/RabbitMQ/cluster/remote actor placement and node-lease features; local TaskSpace claim heartbeat/expiry is in scope.
- A second product-only TaskSpace, actor registry or organization store.

## Verification

Compiled product local init; Ctrl and Data journeys; process interruptions before/after each owner commit; repeat/idempotency; live source modification/deletion; explicit replan; static boundary gates; independent fresh verification and coding AttractorCheck.
