# Track: verify-provider-context-architecture

## Goal

Establish independent, repeatable evidence that provider context construction obeys the mission architecture after canonical replay repair, typed projection separation, coverage gating, and context epoch invalidation.

## Verification Claims

- Chat repair cannot enter Responses replay modules.
- The named canonical compiler preserves every source tool pair and emits complete coverage proof across 100 rounds.
- Removing a valid checkpoint does not change provider-visible stateless replay semantics.
- Epoch mismatch, rewind, fork, incomplete output, and damaged checkpoint paths fail closed to canonical truth.
- The historical incident shape is explained by the former latest-pair projection and is covered without provider network access.

## Non-Goals

- Replaying the historical session against a live provider.
- Introducing repetition thresholds, work-mode heuristics, or a second History authority.
- Weakening safepoint-only full runtime snapshots.
